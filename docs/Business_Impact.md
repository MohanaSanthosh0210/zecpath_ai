# Business Impact Analysis

## Recruitment Challenges Addressed

### Manual Screening

Traditional recruitment requires significant manual effort to review resumes and shortlist candidates.

### Interview Inconsistency

Different interviewers often evaluate candidates differently, leading to inconsistent decisions.

### Slow Hiring Cycle

Recruitment processes can take days or weeks due to multiple screening stages.

## Benefits of Zecpath AI

### Faster Resume Screening

* Automated resume parsing
* ATS-based ranking
* Reduced screening time

### Reduced Manual Effort

* Automated candidate evaluation
* Automated scoring
* Automated recommendations

### Consistent Evaluation

* Standardized scoring logic
* Explainable decision-making
* Reduced human bias

### Scalable Hiring

* Supports large candidate volumes
* Faster shortlisting
* Improved recruiter productivity
