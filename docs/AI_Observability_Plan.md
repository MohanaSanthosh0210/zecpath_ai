# AI Observability Plan

## Objective

Provide complete monitoring and observability for every AI module.

---

## Monitored Modules

- Resume Parser
- ATS Engine
- Screening AI
- HR Interview AI
- Technical Interview AI
- Machine Test AI
- Hiring Intelligence
- Report Generator

---

## Monitoring Goals

- Track response time
- Measure prediction accuracy
- Record failures
- Store model outputs
- Monitor candidate processing
- Track final hiring decisions

---

## Expected Outcome

A centralized monitoring framework capable of tracking AI system performance, reliability, and operational health.
