# Zecpath AI Demo Script

## Introduction

Good morning/afternoon everyone.

Today I will demonstrate Zecpath AI, an AI-powered hiring intelligence platform that automates candidate evaluation from resume screening to final hiring recommendations.

## Step 1 – Resume Upload

The recruiter uploads a candidate resume into the system.

## Step 2 – Resume Parsing

The Resume Parser extracts:

* Candidate details
* Skills
* Experience
* Education
* Certifications

## Step 3 – ATS Evaluation

The ATS Engine compares candidate skills with job requirements and generates an ATS score.

## Step 4 – Screening AI

The Screening AI analyzes candidate responses and produces:

* Intent analysis
* Screening score
* Candidate evaluation

## Step 5 – HR Interview AI

The HR Interview AI evaluates:

* Communication
* Confidence
* Behavioral indicators
* HR interview performance

## Step 6 – Technical Interview AI

The Technical Interview AI evaluates:

* Technical knowledge
* Aptitude
* Problem-solving ability
* Scenario handling

## Step 7 – Decision AI

The Decision AI combines all scores and generates:

* Hiring fit percentage
* Confidence score
* Final recommendation

## Step 8 – Final Hiring Report

The recruiter receives a complete hiring report with explainable recommendations.

## Conclusion

Zecpath AI improves hiring speed, consistency, scalability, and decision quality while reducing manual effort.
