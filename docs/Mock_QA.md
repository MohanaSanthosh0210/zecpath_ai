# Zecpath AI – Mock Question & Answer

## Q1. What problem does Zecpath AI solve?

Zecpath AI automates the recruitment process by performing resume screening, AI-based interviews, and intelligent hiring recommendations.

---

## Q2. Why did you develop this project?

To reduce recruiter workload, improve hiring consistency, and provide explainable AI-driven recruitment.

---

## Q3. What is ATS scoring?

ATS scoring evaluates how well a candidate's resume matches a job description using skills, education, experience, and project relevance.

---

## Q4. How does Resume Parsing work?

The Resume Parser extracts structured information such as personal details, education, skills, projects, certifications, and experience from uploaded resumes.

---

## Q5. How does Screening AI work?

Screening AI conducts the initial evaluation by analyzing candidate responses using NLP techniques and generates a screening score.

---

## Q6. What is the purpose of HR Interview AI?

It evaluates communication, behavioral traits, confidence, professionalism, and HR-specific competencies.

---

## Q7. What does Technical Interview AI evaluate?

It evaluates technical knowledge, coding ability, problem-solving skills, aptitude, and scenario-based reasoning.

---

## Q8. What is Decision AI?

Decision AI combines ATS, Screening, HR, Technical, and behavioral scores to generate the final hiring recommendation.

---

## Q9. How is bias reduced?

The platform uses standardized scoring, governance policies, audit logs, and explainable AI principles to reduce subjectivity.

---

## Q10. How is data secured?

JWT authentication, role-based access control, secure APIs, and audit logging are used to protect sensitive candidate information.

---

## Q11. How is the system monitored?

Monitoring includes API logging, response time tracking, failure monitoring, model performance metrics, and observability dashboards.

---

## Q12. Can the system scale?

Yes. The architecture supports microservices, load balancing, asynchronous processing, caching, and horizontal scaling.

---

## Q13. What datasets were used?

The project includes synthetic resumes, job descriptions, interview responses, ATS results, screening reports, and hiring reports for demonstration.

---

## Q14. What are the future enhancements?

- LLM-powered interviews
- Video interview analysis
- Emotion recognition
- Multilingual interview support
- Enterprise analytics dashboards

---

## Q15. Why is Zecpath AI different?

Zecpath AI provides an end-to-end AI recruitment platform integrating resume parsing, ATS scoring, AI interviews, governance, monitoring, and explainable decision-making within a unified architecture.
